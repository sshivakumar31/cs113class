1. According to the author, if computer science is not about computers, then what is it about?
The algorithm is the chief object of study. It is not about the physical machine.
2. In your own words, what is an algorithm?
It is the set of well-ordered steps or procedures to achieve the specific goal. It is a systematic and unambiguous set of instructions that can be executed by a computing agent.
3. According to the author, why is computer science not a science?
Computers are tools. Science follows the scientific method. Algorithms are abstract and do not use the scientific method as the primary tool to investigate them, so CS is not fundamentally science.
